# Creator settlements

Marketplace prices are expressed in Uzbek so'm (`UZS`) and stored in integer tiyin values. A creator's settlement record contains gross captured amount, platform fee, refunds, adjustments, taxes or required metadata, and net creator share.

Customer payment methods may include Humo, Uzcard, Visa, and Mastercard when the selected acquirer is configured. A creator does not receive a payout merely because an invoice was created; the payment must be captured, reconciled, outside refund windows, and associated with a verified payout profile.

Refunds and disputes create new settlement events. The original capture remains immutable. Settlement exports are available to Platform Admin and are scoped so a creator sees only their own records.
